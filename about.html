About me

I'm a product designer with 15+ years of combined experience across product design, content strategy, UX writing and information architecture.

I started my career designing content systems and simplifying complex technical communication. Over time, I moved into product design, where I shape the full experience — not just what it says, but how it works, scales, and supports people in their daily workflows.

My approach

  1. Interviews, support data and analytics, until the real problem is clear.

  2. Flows and screens from rough to final, tested with users before polishing.

  3. Labels, errors and empty states written with the screens, not after them.

  4. Success metrics agreed up front and checked after launch.

  5. Each release feeds the next round of decisions.

FAQ

I start by understanding technical constraints early and frame design decisions in terms engineers care about: feasibility, performance, and maintainability. I use shared tools like Figma dev mode, provide detailed specs, and maintain open channels for quick questions. Most importantly, I treat engineers as partners in solving user problems, not just implementers of my designs.

I turn ambiguity into clarity through structured discovery. I start with stakeholder interviews to understand the real problem, create user journey maps to identify gaps, and propose small experiments or prototypes to test assumptions quickly. I document everything and get explicit sign-offs on scope to prevent endless revisions.

I look for solutions that serve both by reframing constraints as design challenges. When there's real tension, I use data and user research to make the business case for user-centered decisions. I also propose phased approaches: quick wins that meet business needs while building toward better user experiences later.

I establish success metrics upfront that tie to both user behavior and business outcomes. This includes usability metrics (task completion rates, error rates), engagement metrics (feature adoption, retention), and business metrics (conversion, revenue impact). I also track qualitative feedback and iterate based on what I learn.
