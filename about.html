I started in content systems. Now I design the whole product: how it works and what it says.

I'm a senior product designer at Voyado in Stockholm, building tools retailers use to know and reach their customers, and prototyping in code with AI.

My approach

  1. 01Map the flowMap how people actually work today, to find where it breaks.
  2. 02Design the core interactionShape the main interaction first, with its words, on every platform it lives on.
  3. 03Cut the noiseGroup, batch and remove until only what helps is left.
  4. 04Test and iterateTest versions with real users and let the data decide.

Lately
