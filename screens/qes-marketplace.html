Electronic signatures
Overview
Oneflow offers three signature levels: Simple (SES), Advanced (AdES), and Qualified (QES). These levels define how strongly a participant’s identity is verified:
SESSES
Simple Electronic Signature

Electronic signature without identity verification.

Can be used for:
✓ Low-risk agreements
✓ Informal agreements

Methods available in Oneflow:
✓ Click-to-sign
✓ SMS verification
✓ Mark as signed
✓ Handwritten signature.

Free with all plans
AdESAdES
Advanced Electronic Signature

Signature with verified identity through electronic ID or certificate. Offers a strong legal assurance.

Can be used for:
✓ Business contracts
✓ Sales agreements
✓ Documents requiring verified identity

Methods available in Oneflow:
✓ Full list is provided further on this page.

Pricing details
QESQES
Qualified Electronic Signature

Signature with the highest legal value. Requires strong identity verification and a qualified certificate issued by a Qualified Trust Service Provider.

Can be used for:
✓ EU-regulated documents
✓ Cross-border agreements
✓ High legal assurance contracts.
